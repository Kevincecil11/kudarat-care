Privacy Policy

What personal information KudaratCare collects, why, who it is shared with and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

[TO CONFIRM: legal business name] ("KudaratCare") decides how your personal data is used on this website. Questions about privacy can be sent to our grievance officer, listed at the end of this policy.

Information we collect

Account and orders: your name, email, mobile number and delivery address.

Payments: Razorpay processes your payment. We receive only the payment status and reference, never your card, UPI or bank details.

Consult a Doctor: your name, age, WhatsApp number, email and the concern you describe. This may include health information, so we only collect it with your consent.

Reviews, bulk enquiries and contact messages: the details you enter, and any photos or videos you upload.

Website use: basic technical data such as device, browser and pages visited, collected through cookies and Google Analytics.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, issue GST invoices, run KC Circle rewards, reply to enquiries, publish approved reviews, prevent fraud and improve the website. We send marketing messages only if you agree to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers needed to run the website: Supabase (database and secure storage), Vercel (website hosting), Razorpay (payments), [TO CONFIRM: courier partner] (delivery) and Google Analytics (website statistics).

Consultation requests are shared only with Dr. Aditya Doshi's clinic. We may share information if the law requires it. We never sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax laws require. Consultation requests are kept for [TO CONFIRM: 12 months]. Account details are kept until you ask us to delete your account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls, so customers can only see their own orders and only authorised staff can see enquiries. Review photos and videos stay private until approved.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent and raise a grievance. Contact our grievance officer below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your bag and sign-in working, and analytics cookies to understand how the website is used. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
